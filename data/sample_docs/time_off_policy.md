# Time Off Policy

Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid vacation per year, credited monthly at a rate
of 1.25 days per month. Unused vacation carries over into the next year up to a maximum
of 10 days; anything beyond that is forfeited at year end.

Employees must request time off at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than
three days. Shorter absences can be requested with less notice, subject to manager
approval.

Sick leave is separate from vacation and is not capped; employees should notify their
manager as soon as they know they will be out. A doctor's note is required only for
absences longer than five consecutive days.

Parental leave provides 12 weeks of paid leave for the primary caregiver and 6 weeks for
the secondary caregiver, in addition to any vacation or sick leave already accrued.
