# New Hire Onboarding Guide

On your first day, IT will provision your laptop and accounts. You should receive an
email with temporary credentials before 9:00 AM. If you do not receive it, contact the IT
helpdesk directly rather than waiting.

New hires complete a two-week onboarding program that covers company history, security
training, and role-specific shadowing. Security training must be completed within the
first five business days; access to internal systems is suspended until it is done.

Every new hire is assigned a buddy from their team who is not their manager. The buddy's
role is to answer day-to-day questions and help the new hire navigate internal tools
during the first month.

Benefits enrollment, including health insurance and retirement contributions, must be
completed within 30 days of the start date. Missing this window means waiting until the
next open enrollment period, except for qualifying life events.
