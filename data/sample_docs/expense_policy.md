# Expense Reimbursement Policy

All business expenses must be submitted through the expense system within 30 days of the
purchase date. Expenses submitted after 30 days require written approval from a director.

Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to $75 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable
under any circumstances. Employees traveling internationally may submit expenses in local
currency; the system converts amounts using the exchange rate on the date of purchase.

Home office equipment, such as desks, chairs, and monitors, is reimbursed up to $500 per
year and is tracked separately from travel expenses. See the Remote Work Policy for
details on that program.

Any single expense over $1,000 requires pre-approval from a director before the purchase
is made, not after. Expenses without pre-approval may be rejected even if they are
otherwise policy-compliant.
