# Remote Work Policy

Employees at Acme Robotics may work remotely up to four days per week, provided their
manager approves the arrangement in writing. Anyone working remotely for more than two
consecutive weeks must notify the People Operations team so payroll tax jurisdictions can
be verified.

Remote employees are expected to be reachable during core hours, which run from 10:00 AM
to 3:00 PM in their local time zone. Outside of core hours, employees may set their own
schedule as long as they meet project deadlines.

Acme Robotics reimburses up to $500 per year for home office equipment, including desks,
chairs, and monitors. Requests for reimbursement go through the standard expense system
and require a receipt.

Employees who relocate to a different country must inform People Operations at least six
weeks before the move, since international remote work can trigger new tax and visa
requirements.
